About

Step by step — at any scale

I grew up in a small village and learned early to move forward one step at a time. Then came Moscow — and numbers at a growing scale: a federal research center, the consolidated budget of one of the world's largest metro systems, payroll for 640 employees at a state energy corporation.

Moving to the U.S. was my biggest project yet. I handled my family's immigration case myself — documents, deadlines, agencies — and brought my whole family here.

Big projects don't scare me: any scale is a sequence of clear steps. Now in South Florida, I'm doing what I've always done — learning the new system step by step and turning complex numbers into clear answers.
